# RegionalSim 0.3.0-dev

**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Status:** Provisional survey-anchored **operational availability**; no morbidity, mortality, structural damage, or independently validated long-run cutoff.

## Context (blinded developer)

After the catastrophic-capability review in
[`../drafts/model-catastrophic-effects.md`](../drafts/model-catastrophic-effects.md)
(0.2.0-dev baseline), the following instruction was given:

> let's not model death - people also don't die so easily and will often find alternative ways to survive, but let's find a calibrated threshold at which people, buildings and companies stop being available for the modeled purposes. there should be evidence from existing shocks (excluding medical effects as specified).

## What changed from 0.2.0-dev

- **Module:** `regional_sim/availability.py` — household temporary relocation (local presence), retail grid-dependent operation, site grid access; **no** death, illness, or building condemnation.
- **Benchmarks / fit:** `benchmarks/availability_observations.json`, `calibration/availability_results.json`, `scripts/validate_availability.py`.
- **Docs:** `docs/operational_availability.md` (methods, sources, gaps); updated `docs/technical.md`, `docs/limitations.md`, `reports/validation.md`.
- **CLI:** `--no-availability` ablation; availability extension on by default.
- **Engine / policy:** relocation trips, return after restoration, store `store_site_usable` / `store_operational`; outside-region needs logged without claiming delivery.
- **Tests:** 25 unit tests (was 19).

0.2.0-dev shock proxy calibration (AEP / ACS commute) is retained; see `docs/calibration.md`.

## Calibrated anchors (stated-intent proxies, not held-out history)

Sources and caveats: `docs/operational_availability.md`.

| Target | Source (summary) | Modeled lever | Three-seed check (2.4k hh, 120 stores) |
|--------|------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| 33% would relocate (1-day outage scenario) | Sue Wing et al. 2025, ComEd survey | Relocation propensity curve vs consecutive home outage hours | ~32.9% away (24h blackout) |
| 83% would relocate (14-day scenario) | Same | Cap / interpolation to 336h | ~83.0% away (336h blackout) |
| 49% SMB would close (1-day scenario) | Same | 49% Bernoulli loss of power-dependent retail ops | ~45.8% power-closed day 1 (sampling noise on n=120) |

Historical benchmarks (2003 blackout firm closures, Hurricane Matthew reopening, Sandy occupancy) are documented but **not** used as fit targets—denominator and exposure mismatch.

## Explicit non-goals

- Mortality, health deterioration, or deprivation→death thresholds.
- Permanent building uninhabitability or asset destruction from systemic stress alone.
- Automatic bankruptcy / permanent firm exit from balances.
- A generic physical “building unavailable” cutoff (not identifiable from cited sources).

## Gaps (headline)

- 0–24h and 24–336h relocation curve shape is C-level except survey endpoints; beyond 336h propensity capped at 0.83.
- Relocation is **engine scripted**, not yet on the replaceable policy API (`--no-availability` for ablation).
- 49% retail rule is stated-intent transfer from Illinois SMB survey, not observed Columbus outage closures.
- Still **not** a world-freeze release under [`../INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../INSTRUCTIONS.md).
