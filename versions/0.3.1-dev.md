# RegionalSim 0.3.1-dev

**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Status:** Participant-policy-controlled relocation/return; provisional survey-anchored availability retained; **not** independently validated or frozen.

## Instruction to the coding agent

Verbatim follow-up (relative to 0.3.0-dev):

> relocation should be a participant decision. do participants decisions depend on communication with other participants and if so what evidence is this based on?

## What changed from 0.3.0-dev

- **Relocation / return** move from engine automation to **`ScriptedPolicy`** (and any replacement policy): `Action('relocate')`, `Action('return')`, `Action('review')`; engine enforces travel, deferral, cancellation, local-activity limits only.
- **Observations** extended with outage history, availability flags, private relocation preference threshold, message exposure counts.
- **Audit doc:** `docs/participant_communication.md` — what peer communication actually changes in the baseline vs what replacement policies *may* use.
- **Tests:** 32 unit tests (was 25), including no-move policy, alternative move policy, no forced relocation from power announcements, two-message stockout adoption regression.
- **Provenance:** 69 register entries (per package README).

0.2.0-dev shock calibration and 0.3.0-dev availability anchors (Sue Wing et al. stated-intent fit) are retained; see `docs/calibration.md`, `docs/operational_availability.md`, `calibration/availability_results.json`.

## Communication: what the baseline uses evidence for

| Behavior | Peer communication in baseline? | Evidence basis |
|----------|----------------------------------|--------------|
| Grocery target bump after **two** independent stockout signals (threshold 2) | **Yes** — delivered messages change requested stock from 2.5→4 days of need | Centola (2010) complex contagion *as mechanism inspiration*; thresholds/probabilities are **C-level**, not fitted from that experiment. Sadus et al. (2023) stockpiling norms: associative context only, not a fitted coefficient. |
| Temporary relocation / return | **No** — driven by private outage observation + survey-anchored preference threshold | Sue Wing et al. (2025) stated preferences; elapsed-outage mapping is C-level. Source does **not** identify neighbor-message effects on relocation. |
| Payments, commute, clinic, medication, store choice | **No** in baseline | — |
| Institutional `power_interruption` feed | Delivered to inbox | Does **not** override household power observation or force relocation |

Full mechanism list and gaps: [`../regional_sim/docs/participant_communication.md`](../regional_sim/docs/participant_communication.md).

## Availability checks (policy-controlled)

Three-seed `validate_availability` (seeds 41–43): ~32.6% away at 24h and ~83.0% at 336h vs 33% / 83% survey anchors; all return by scenario end in stress test. See `calibration/availability_results.json`.

## Gaps (headline)

- No source-derived coefficients linking peer messages to relocation, payments, or mobility.
- Relocation costs, destination capacity, outside-region lodging/supply still unresolved boundary.
- Policy-independence gap from 0.3.0-dev **closed** for relocation; communication-to-behavior remains narrow (stockout adoption only).
